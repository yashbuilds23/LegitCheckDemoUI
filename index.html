Assessment-2 Methodology

Design Thinking Framework: Problem to Ideation

A structured walkthrough showing how student empathy interviews shaped LEGITCHECK's feature requirements.

01. Problem Statement

Students get offers on WhatsApp, Instagram, and email but cannot easily tell genuine from deceptive. Generic “looks suspicious” warnings give no next step.

Four Critical Unanswered Questions:

1. Is the organisation genuine?

2. Is the recruiter really linked to it?

3. Is the specific offer legitimate?

4. What evidence should be checked before acting?

02. How Might We (HMW)

“How might we help students evaluate suspicious online opportunities, understand the evidence, and take safer next steps, without a false guarantee?”

This framing prioritizes clarity over automated binary judgment—empowering students to perform guided checks rather than trusting black-box AI scores.

Root Cause Hypothesis

The 5 Whys Analysis (PPT Slide 08)

PPT Assessment 2
WHY 1

Why might a student trust a suspicious offer?

Its presentation and visual polish appear convincing.

WHY 2

Why might that presentation be persuasive?

Polish, branding, and urgency imply legitimacy.

WHY 3

Why might the impression go unchallenged?

Sender identity and corporate design are easily imitated.

WHY 4

Why might verification be skipped?

Checking organisation, recruiter, and offer details takes time and effort across scattered sources.

WHY 5

Why does the problem persist?

No simple, explainable way to verify at the exact moment of decision.

Working Root-Cause Hypothesis: Verification friction under uncertainty and time pressure. LEGITCHECK lowers this friction by bringing evidence and actions together into one interface.
Proto-Persona (PPT Slide 06)

Jayesh, 19 — First-Year College Student

01. GOALS

Discover genuine career opportunities without falling for deceptive fees or phishing.

02. NEEDS

Understand clear warning signs and actionable verification steps before taking action.

03. FRUSTRATIONS

Unclear recruiter identities, pressure tactics, and scattered evidence across sites.

04. SUCCESS CRITERIA

Understand the evidence available and feel confident in making a safer next choice.

Jayesh's Empathy Map Matrix (PPT Slide 05)

SAYS

  • “Is this internship real?”
  • “Should I pay to confirm my seat?”
  • “Does anyone know this company?”

THINKS

  • “It looks professional, but why the rush?”
  • “What if I miss a genuine chance?”
  • “Who is actually messaging me?”

DOES

  • Re-reads the message text
  • Searches the company name online
  • Asks friends or WhatsApp college groups

FEELS

  • Excited by the opportunity
  • Pressured by tight deadlines
  • Worried about losing money and personal data
